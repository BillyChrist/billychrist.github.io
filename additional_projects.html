Frame Design

Modular frame designed for effecient iterations, made possible by easily configurable attatment mechanisms. The main challenge for this design was with the mounting brackets. Since the components had to be fabricated using a 3D printer and limited to PLA, careful consideration was given to the direction of force with respect to the orientation of the layer lines.
