Lunar Whale

Autonomous VTOL Drone Testbed

Autonomous VTOL drone testbed designed to conduct experiments with airfoil design.

Airfoil Design

The airfoil design was based on the NACA 4412-IL airfoil. This design was chosen based of the stable CL/CD though a wide range of AoA before stall. With a +- 9 [deg] AoA before stall, this design allowed the highest safety margin during vertical and horizontal flight transitions.

  • pitch range vs tilt transition

  • Design & fabrication

  • Materials
    • --Foaming ligthweight PLA. Designed specifically to be printed spiralized, meaning there are no retractions, and no support material required. This ensures a clean print the best possiblility for eliminating surface anomalies and extraneous material.
  • Coatings & treatment
    • -- Printed airfoils were coated with a thin layer of white paint to minimize absortivity and emissivity, while also ensuring a smooth surface for minimal drag. This coating also served to fill in any layer gaps and prevent fatigue and creep failures.

Frame Design

Modular frame designed for effecient iterations, made possible by easily configurable attatment mechanisms. The main challenge for this design was with the mounting brackets. Since the components had to be fabricated using a 3D printer and limited to PLA, careful consideration was given to the direction of force with respect to the orientation of the layer lines.
